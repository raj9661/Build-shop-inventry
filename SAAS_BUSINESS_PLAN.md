## 🏗️ Platform Architecture

### **Multi-Tenant Structure**
```
Platform Owner (You)
├── Customer 1 (SUPER_DUPER_ADMIN)
│   ├── Shop 1 (SUPER_ADMIN manages)
│   ├── Shop 2 (ADMIN manages)
│   └── Users (STAFF, USER roles)
├── Customer 2 (SUPER_DUPER_ADMIN)
│   └── Their shops and users
└── Platform Moderators
    ├── Account management
    ├── Violation handling
    └── Support
```

### **Role Hierarchy**
1. **PLATFORM_OWNER** - You (platform management)
2. **SUPER_DUPER_ADMIN** - Customer who purchased subscription
3. **SUPER_ADMIN** - Customer's top admin
4. **ADMIN** - Customer's admin
5. **USER** - Customer's user
6. **STAFF** - Customer's staff
7. **MODERATOR** - Platform moderator
8. **CREATOR** - Platform content creator
